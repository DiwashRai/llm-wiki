# Problem statement

Do I build the AgentOS/Software factory backend myself or try to use open source?

## Decision

Build myself

### Opportunity cost

Time and effort on the actual implementation

### Reasons for decision

- It's a two way door decision
- Nothing pre-existing matches requirements directly
- Get more experience using agentic development for real projects

**Second order consequences**

- Nice to have
  - Project gets adopted more widely and easily due to no costs or complex dependencies
  - We can quickly adapt it as our own requirements change
- To avoid
  - Maintenance of project takes a lot of time
  - Backend control plane doesn't actually affect our overall productivity much
